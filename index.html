01 / KNOW THE THREATS

Spot the scam.
Stop the harm.

Cybercriminals use urgency, fear and attractive offers to trick people. Learn the warning signs before you respond.

✉
MESSAGES & EMAIL

Phishing

Fake messages or websites that imitate trusted organisations to steal passwords, OTPs or banking details.

₹
MONEY & PAYMENTS

UPI / Payment Fraud

Scammers trick people into approving collect requests, sharing PINs or scanning malicious QR codes.

◉
IDENTITY & PRIVACY

Identity Theft

Personal information is misused to impersonate you, open accounts or commit fraud in your name.

▣
SOCIAL PLATFORMS

Social Media Scams

Fake profiles, impersonation, suspicious giveaways and malicious links spread through social networks.

⌁
CALLS & IMPERSONATION

Digital Arrest Scams

Fraudsters pose as officials and use threats, video calls or fabricated allegations to extort money.

▤
DEVICES & DATA

Malware & Ransomware

Harmful software can spy on activity, steal information, lock files or disrupt your device.

BEFORE YOU REPORT

Keep these details ready

Share information only through official reporting channels or directly with authorised police personnel.

  • ✓ Date and approximate time of incident
  • ✓ Transaction ID / UTR, if applicable
  • ✓ Screenshots and original messages
  • ✓ Phone numbers, email IDs or website links involved
  • ✓ Bank / payment provider details, if relevant
